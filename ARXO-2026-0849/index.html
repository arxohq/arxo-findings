ARXO-2026-0849 · Reported

Department of Health and Human Services: 34 references to former U.S. Code sections or former act names (45 CFR)

In the CFR chapters assigned to the Department of Health and Human Services in 45 CFR, 34 references in 32 sections use a former address or name: 27 cite a section transferred to another section; 5 cite a section replaced by a new one when the title was enacted as positive law; 2 cite a repealed section. Each reference was compared with release point 119-134 of the U.S. Code, which names where the old number went.

Consequence

A reader who follows these references finds a note that the section moved, or no section, instead of the text. A reference to a moved section may keep its legal effect, but the address no longer leads to the text. No effect on a decision or an amount has been established.

Proposed correction

The agency decides how to update each reference. Where the U.S. Code names the section the old number moved to, the evidence below gives it.
