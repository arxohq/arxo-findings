ARXO-2026-0094 · Reported

MAR 5.5.2, 6.1.7, 6.2.2 and 6.4.3: references printed as "MAR 5.2.65", "MAR 6.2.21" and "MAR 5.2.6.6" name no existing rule

Four references in the market abuse and disclosure chapters name rules that do not exist: "MAR 5.2.65" (5.5.2 and 6.1.7), "MAR 6.2.21" (6.2.2) and "MAR 5.2.6.6" (6.4.3). Up to V4 (14 February 2019) these places read "MAR 5.2.6" and "MAR 6.2.2"; the merged numbers first appear in V5 (2019), and we found no amending instrument that made that change. Amendments No. 6 (2020) shows "5.2.5" as new text in 6.1.7, next to a broken field code ("Error! Reference source not found."), and its text of 6.2.2 and 6.4.3 is also damaged; V7 to V13 still print "MAR 5.2.65". The AFSA online text is the same. The bracketed titles show the intended targets for three of the places: 5.2.5 (Unlawful disclosure of Inside Information) in 5.5.2 and 6.1.7, and 6.2.1 (Obligation to draw up insider lists) in 6.2.2. In 6.4.3 there is no title.

Proposed correction

Please correct the references to "MAR 5.2.5" in 5.5.2 and 6.1.7 and to "MAR 6.2.1" in 6.2.2; because the bracketed titles fix the meaning, this can be an editorial correction under section 19(3)(a) of the AIFC Regulations on AIFC Acts. Please also state which rule 6.4.3 is meant to refer to.
