ARXO-2026-0130 · Reported

BPG not updated: it says Credit Providers are not Banking Business firms and excludes them from the leverage ratio, which BBR 4.36 has applied to Providing Credit since December 2022

BPG paragraph 6 says: "Credit Providers as defined in BBR 1.7 are not included in Banking Business firms according to BBR 1.4." BBR 1.4(3) has said since 2018 that a Banking Business firm may be a Credit Provider, and BPG paragraphs 2, 4 and 5 themselves include Credit Providers. The BPG tables also exclude Credit Providers from Section 4F (Rules 4.36-4.37, leverage ratio). That matched BBR 4.36 until the version with amendments as of 9 December 2022, which applies Section 4F to firms licensed for "Accepting Deposits" or "Providing Credit". The BPG (28 January 2019) has not been updated. The BBR prevail, so the problem is misleading guidance.

Proposed correction

Please delete BPG paragraph 6 and remove the Credit Provider exclusion from Section 4F (Rules 4.36-4.37) in the BPG tables.
