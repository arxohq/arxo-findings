ARXO-2026-0844 · Reported

Department of Energy: 46 references to former U.S. Code sections or former act names

In the CFR chapters assigned to the Department of Energy, 46 references in 38 sections use a former address or name: 19 cite a section replaced by a new one when the title was enacted as positive law; 11 cite a repealed section; 9 cite a section transferred to another section; 6 cite a former appendix not carried in the current Code; 1 cites a renumbered section. Each reference was compared with release point 119-134 of the U.S. Code, which names where the old number went.

Consequence

A reader who follows these references finds a note that the section moved, or no section, instead of the text. A reference to a moved section may keep its legal effect, but the address no longer leads to the text. No effect on a decision or an amount has been established.

Proposed correction

The agency decides how to update each reference. Where the U.S. Code names the section the old number moved to, the evidence below gives it.
