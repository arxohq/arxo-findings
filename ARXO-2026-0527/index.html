ARXO-2026-0527 · Reported

National Science Foundation: 12 references to 4 CFR parts 101 to 105, removed in 2000

In the CFR chapters assigned to the National Science Foundation, 12 references in 6 sections cite 4 CFR parts 101 to 105 or sections in those parts. A final rule removed these parts, chapter II of title 4, effective December 22, 2000 (65 FR 70405). The eCFR of October 27, 2026 has only chapter I in title 4 and shows PARTS 84-199 [RESERVED].

Consequence

A reader who follows these references finds no text at the cited address. No effect on a decision or an amount has been established.

Proposed correction

The agency decides how to update each reference. The revised standards are at 31 CFR chapter IX, parts 900 to 904 (65 FR 70390). We did not map sections one to one.
