ARXO-2026-0960 · Accepted by the addressee

SR XML: 38 articles carry the same element identifier (eId) as another article

In the XML of 7 acts of the Classified Compilation (SR), 38 articles carry the same element identifier (eId) as another article of the same act, so a link to that identifier does not lead to one article. The PDF of the same version prints each article once under its own number.

Consequence

A link or a machine reader that uses the element identifier may reach the other article that carries it. The PDF, which is authoritative, is not affected.

Proposed correction

The Federal Chancellery decides how to correct each place; the evidence gives the wording of each language version.
