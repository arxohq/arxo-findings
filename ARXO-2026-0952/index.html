ARXO-2026-0952 · Accepted by the addressee

SR footnotes: in 13 articles one language version gives another year or page for the same amending act

In 13 articles of 10 acts of the Classified Compilation (SR), the footnotes of the German, French and Italian versions describe the same amending act with the same date, but one version cites a different year or page of the Official Compilation (AS). Each finding was read in the PDF of the consolidated version, which is the authoritative form. Which language version is right is not decided here.

Consequence

A reader who follows the footnote of one language version reaches a different entry of the Official Compilation than readers of the other versions, or none. No effect on the content of the provisions has been established.

Proposed correction

The Federal Chancellery decides how to correct each place; the evidence gives the wording of each language version.
