ARXO-2026-0122 · Reported

BBR cites BPG Chapter 9 Sections D, E and F for the LCR, NSFR and maturity mismatch; in the BPG these are Sections G, H and I

BBR 9.16(5)(b) (the AFSA's power to adjust "requirements under section D of Chapter 9 of the BPG" for HQLA and net cash outflows), 9.19(2) (NSFR methodology, "Section E", twice) and 9.21(1)(a) (maturity-ladder methodology, "section F") cite BPG Chapter 9 by section letter; the notes to 9.16(3) and 9.21 do the same. In the published BPG (AFSA-L-GB-2019-0001, 28 January 2019) Sections D, E and F of Chapter 9 are Funding Strategy, Stress Testing and Contingency Funding Plan; the LCR, NSFR and Maturity Mismatch Approach are Sections G, H and I. The right sections can be found by their titles.

Proposed correction

Please read Section G for Section D (9.16(5)(b) and the note to 9.16(3)), Section H for Section E (9.19(2), twice) and Section I for Section F (9.21(1)(a) and the note to 9.21).
