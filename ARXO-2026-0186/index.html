ARXO-2026-0186 · Reported

Foundations Regulations s.36: since Amendments No. 1 (2019) "the Foundation's return date" is not defined; since Amendments No. 3 (2023) s.36(4)-(5) still refer to "Schedule 3 (Fines and Fees)" and to fees "prescribed in Regulations"

Amendments No. 1 to the Foundations Regulations (2019) deleted the definition of the return date ("each anniversary of the date it received its Commercial Licence") from section 36(3) but kept the words "made up to the Foundation's return date"; the term is not defined elsewhere. The filing deadline, "within 6 months of the end of each financial year", does not depend on that date, and the Registrar may set another date. Amendments No. 3 (2023) moved fees to the Fees Rules (section 61) and removed fees from Schedule 3, but section 36(4) still refers to "Schedule 3 (Fines and Fees)" and section 36(5) still requires "application for registration fees ... as prescribed in Regulations".

Proposed correction

Please specify the date to which an annual return under section 36 must be made up, and update the fee references in section 36(4) and (5) to match the current fee provisions.
