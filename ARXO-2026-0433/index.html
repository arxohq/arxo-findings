ARXO-2026-0433 · Reported

Rome I, Maltese version: Article 8(4) refers to paragraph 2 or 2a, and there is no paragraph 2a

The other versions of the escape clause for employment contracts refer to paragraphs 2 or 3. Article 8 has no paragraph 2a.

Consequence

In the Maltese text the comparison with a more closely connected country leaves out the country of paragraph 3 (the place of business that engaged the employee) and names a paragraph that does not exist.

Proposed correction

Replace 2a with 3 in Maltese Article 8(4).

Ruma I, verżjoni Maltija: l-Artikolu 8(4) jirreferi għall-paragrafu 2 jew 2a, u m'hemmx paragrafu 2a

Il-verżjonijiet l-oħra tal-klawżola liberatorja għall-kuntratti ta' impjieg jirreferu għall-paragrafi 2 jew 3. L-Artikolu 8 m'għandux paragrafu 2a.

Consequence

Fit-test Malti, it-tqabbil ma' pajjiż li miegħu l-kuntratt ikun aktar marbut iħalli barra l-pajjiż indikat fil-paragrafu 3 (fejn ikun jinsab in-negozju li fih ġie impjegat l-impjegat) u jsemmi paragrafu li ma jeżistix.

Proposed correction

Fl-Artikolu 8(4) tal-verżjoni Maltija, 2a jinbidel b'3.
