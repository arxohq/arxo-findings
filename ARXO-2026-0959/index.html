SR: in 5 places of 2 acts an expression replaced throughout the act remains in the consolidated version

In 5 places of 2 acts of the Classified Compilation (SR), an amending act replaced an expression throughout the act, but the consolidated version of one language still contains the former expression: «l’âge ordinaire de l’AVS» → «l’âge de référence» in SR 172.220.141.1 (AS 2023 774); «l’âge AVS» → «l’âge de référence» in SR 172.220.141.1 (AS 2023 774); «esame successivo» → «controllo periodico» in SR 741.41 (AS 2025 646). Each finding was read in the PDF of the consolidated version, which is the authoritative form.

Consequence

The consolidated version shows an expression that the amending act replaced. A reader of that language version reads a wording that is no longer in force. No effect on a decision has been established.

Proposed correction

The Federal Chancellery decides how to correct each place; the evidence gives the wording of each language version.
