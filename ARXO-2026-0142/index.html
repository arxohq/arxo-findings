ARXO-2026-0142 · Reported

"Insurance Manager" (an addressee of PRU(INT)) has no definition; PRU(INT) 1.3(2) uses "Insurance Mediation" instead of the GEN activity "Insurance Intermediation"

PRU(INT) 1.3(1) applies the rules to "Insurance Intermediaries and Insurance Managers". The Glossary defines Insurance Intermediary but has no entry for Insurance Manager, and GEN defines the activity "Insurance Management" (Schedule 1, paragraph 24) but not the term "Insurance Manager". The addressee can be identified as a firm licensed for Insurance Management, but no text says so; the AFSA's Guidance to the AIFC Captive Insurance Regime states that "Insurance Manager is defined in the AIFC General Rules". Separately, 1.3(2) speaks of "an Authorisation to carry on Insurance Mediation"; the Regulated Activity is "Insurance Intermediation" (GEN Schedule 1, paragraph 15).

Proposed correction

Please add a Glossary definition, for example "Insurance Manager: an Authorised Firm whose Licence authorises it to carry on the Regulated Activity of Insurance Management", and read "Insurance Intermediation" for "Insurance Mediation" in PRU(INT) 1.3(2).
