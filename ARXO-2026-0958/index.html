SR 631.144.0: merged citation «RU 2007 14691» in an Italian footnote

In the Italian version of SR 631.144.0, Art. 9 para. 3, a footnote cites «RU 2007 14691». The footnote links lead to two entries of the Official Compilation, AS 2007 1 and AS 2007 1469: the two citations have been merged into one. Each finding was read in the PDF of the consolidated version, which is the authoritative form.

Consequence

The merged citation leads to no entry of the Official Compilation; the reader has to find the two entries from the links. No effect on the content of the provision.

Proposed correction

The Federal Chancellery decides how to correct each place; the evidence gives the wording of each language version.
