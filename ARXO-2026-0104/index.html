ARXO-2026-0104 · Reported

AFSA online Market Rules do not contain MAR 1.9.3 and 1.9.4 (Amendments No. 7, 29 June 2022)

The AFSA online Market Rules do not contain MAR 1.9.3 (Expert’s responsibility) and 1.9.4 (Exceptions from liability), which Amendments No. 7 inserted with effect from 29 June 2022. In the online text, 1.9.2 (Limitations on the application of MAR 1.9.1) is followed directly by 1.10 (AIFC ESG Debentures). The consolidated versions V8 to V13 on aifc.kz contain both rules. The online text applied another change made by the same instrument (MAR 2.1(2), which excludes Listed Funds and Exempt Offerors from MAR 2), but not its change to MAR 3.1-1(1), which in the act also excludes an Exempt Offeror from MAR 3. The online Schedule 4, paragraph 1(a)(ii) still refers to "consent under MAR 1.9.3", and the Glossary defines "Expert" by MAR 1.9.3; a reader of the online rulebook cannot find that rule. Our earlier observation on the Glossary entry "Expert" (AIFC-GLO-06) relied on the online text and is withdrawn: the definition resolves in the act. MAR 1.9.4(4)(a) has a separate wrong reference (see AIFC2-MKT-05).

Proposed correction

Please insert MAR 1.9.3 and 1.9.4 in the online Market Rules and apply the change to MAR 3.1-1(1) made by Amendments No. 7.
