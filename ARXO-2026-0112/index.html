ARXO-2026-0112 · Reported

Reports on Single Family Office and Corporate Treasury Centre Funds: the draft extends CIS 10.4 and 10.5 to every Domestic Fund, but 10.5(b) and the Guidance to 2.4.17 do not match

Under the current CIS, reports on Single Family Office and Corporate Treasury Centre Funds (10.5-1(j), 10.5-1.1) have no deadline, frequency or recipient: both Funds must be Exempt Funds, and 10.5, which sets the timing and recipients of Fund reports, applies to Non-Exempt Funds only (10.1(ii)). CIS also does not require these Funds to be audited (10.4 and 10.1(i)). The draft in Consultation Paper AFSA-PSRD-CSP-2026-0001 extends the audit rule in 10.4 and the periodic reports in 10.5(a) to each Domestic Fund, which answers most of this point. Two questions remain on the draft. First, 10.5(b) still sets the four-month deadline for the annual report "for the Non-Exempt Fund", while 10.5(c) no longer says "Non-Exempt", so the deadline for the annual report of other Domestic Funds is unclear. Second, the Guidance to the new 2.4.17 says that for a Corporate Treasury Centre Fund "the standard Fund Manager reporting requirements under CIS 10.5 also do not apply", while the draft 10.5(a) covers each Domestic Fund and states no exception.

Proposed correction

When finalising the draft, please make 10.5(b) cover every Fund to which 10.5(a) applies (for example "for the Fund", as in 10.5(c)), and either state in the rules that 10.5 does not apply to Corporate Treasury Centre Funds, with the reporting that replaces it, or align the Guidance to 2.4.17 with 10.5(a).
