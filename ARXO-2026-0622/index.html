T4127 (123rd edition), Option 2, Prince Edward Island: "Form TD1NL" instead of "Form TD1PE"

In Chapter 5 (Option 2) of the T4127 edition effective July 1, 2026, the Prince Edward Island formula says: "TCP = The total of personal non-refundable tax credits amounts reported on Form TD1NL. If Form TD1NL is not filed, TCP is $15,000." TD1NL is the Newfoundland and Labrador form; the paragraph before it is for Newfoundland and Labrador. The amount $15,000 is the Prince Edward Island basic amount in Table 8.2, so only the form name is wrong.

Consequence

No amount changes. A developer who reads Option 2 for Prince Edward Island is pointed to the form of another province.

Proposed correction

Form TD1PE (twice) in the Prince Edward Island paragraph of Chapter 5.
