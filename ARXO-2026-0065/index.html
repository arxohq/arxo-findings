ARXO-2026-0065 · Reported

Glossary, "Operating a Loan Crowdfunding Platform": points to Schedule 3 of the Framework Regulations, which no longer exists

The entry reads "The Market Activity as defined in paragraph 4 of Schedule 3 of the Framework Regulations". Market Activities moved to Schedule 4 of the General Rules (Framework Regulations section 18(1)); the neighbouring entries were updated, this one was not.

Proposed correction

"The Market Activity defined in paragraph 4 of Schedule 4 of the AIFC General Rules."
