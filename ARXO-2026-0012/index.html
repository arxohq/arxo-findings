ARXO-2026-0012 · Accepted by the addressee

eCFR shows $7,455 in 11 CFR 111.24(b); the January 2025 rule sets $7,445

Published at a date promised in advance to the press (2026-10-28T11:00:00-04:00); the responsible institutions had been told earlier, as listed below.

The same 2025 rule replaces the first amount in paragraph (b) with $7,445; this is also the figure its table derives from $7,256. The eCFR shows $7,455 in its versions as of 31 December 2025 and 20 October 2026: the amendment was incorporated with two digits transposed.

Consequence

The eCFR overstates the maximum penalty under paragraph (b) by $10. No penalty actually assessed on the eCFR figure is claimed.

Proposed correction

$7,445, as set by the rule.
