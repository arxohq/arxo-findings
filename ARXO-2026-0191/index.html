ARXO-2026-0191 · Reported

aifc.kz: the page of the CIT/VAT-exempt List sends readers for the 'current version' to the regulator's page of a different act — the AML list of services subject to financial monitoring

The aifc.kz page "The List of financial services provided by the AIFC participants, income from which is exempt from CIT and VAT" (Joint Order No. 126/547/118 of 2020) says that the current version can be found on the regulator's website and links to orderly.myafsa.com/articles/the-list-of-financial-services-and-ancillary-services-...-subject-to-the-financial-monitoring. That address is the regulator's page of a different act: the list of services subject to financial monitoring, which aifc.kz publishes on its own page with the same link.

Proposed correction

Please remove the "current version" link from this page, or point it to a page that reproduces the 2020 Joint Order.
