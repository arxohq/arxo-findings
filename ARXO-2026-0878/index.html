ARXO-2026-0878 · Reported

Department of Transportation: 234 references to former U.S. Code sections or former act names (14 CFR, chapter I, part 27)

In the CFR chapters assigned to the Department of Transportation in 14 CFR, chapter I, part 27, 234 references in 46 sections use a former address or name: 234 cite a section replaced by a new one when the title was enacted as positive law. Each reference was compared with release point 119-134 of the U.S. Code, which names where the old number went.

Consequence

A reader who follows these references finds a note that the section moved, or no section, instead of the text. A reference to a moved section may keep its legal effect, but the address no longer leads to the text. No effect on a decision or an amount has been established.

Proposed correction

The agency decides how to update each reference. Where the U.S. Code names the section the old number moved to, the evidence below gives it.
