ARXO-2026-0964 · Reported

SR 232.121 (DesV), Art. 23 para. 4: the German and Italian versions refer to Art. 22 para. 2, the French version to Art. 21 para. 2

Art. 23 para. 4 of the Design Ordinance (DesV) refers to Art. 22 para. 2 in the German and Italian versions and to Art. 21 para. 2 in the French version. Both paragraphs exist: Art. 22 para. 2 concerns the separate keeping of supporting documents, Art. 21 para. 2 the extension of protection for a multiple deposit. The divergence is already in the original text in the Official Compilation (AS 2002 1122) in all three languages.

Consequence

Readers of different language versions are sent to different provisions; at most one of them can be the one intended. No effect on a decision has been established.

Proposed correction

The authors of the act decide which reference is intended; the evidence gives the wording of each language version.

Deutsch

SR 232.121 (DesV), Art. 23 Abs. 4: deutsch und italienisch Verweis auf Art. 22 Abs. 2, französisch auf Art. 21 Abs. 2

Die deutsche und die italienische Fassung von Art. 23 Abs. 4 DesV verweisen auf Art. 22 Abs. 2, die französische auf Art. 21 Abs. 2. Beide Absätze bestehen: Art. 22 Abs. 2 regelt die Aussonderung von Beweisurkunden, Art. 21 Abs. 2 die Verlängerung des Schutzes bei einer Sammelhinterlegung. Die Abweichung steht bereits im ursprünglichen Text in der AS (AS 2002 1122) in allen drei Sprachen.

Français

RS 232.121 (ODes), art. 23, al. 4 : les versions allemande et italienne renvoient à l'art. 22, al. 2, la version française à l'art. 21, al. 2

L'art. 23, al. 4, de l'ordonnance sur les designs renvoie à l'art. 22, al. 2, dans les versions allemande et italienne et à l'art. 21, al. 2, dans la version française. Les deux alinéas existent : l'art. 22, al. 2, règle le classement à part des documents justificatifs, l'art. 21, al. 2, la prolongation de la protection en cas de dépôt multiple. La divergence figure déjà dans le texte original publié au RO (RO 2002 1122) dans les trois langues.

it

RS 232.121 (ODes), art. 23 cpv. 4: le versioni tedesca e italiana rinviano all'art. 22 cpv. 2, la versione francese all'art. 21 cpv. 2

L'art. 23 cpv. 4 dell'ordinanza sul design rinvia all'art. 22 cpv. 2 nelle versioni tedesca e italiana e all'art. 21 cpv. 2 nella versione francese. Entrambi i capoversi esistono: l'art. 22 cpv. 2 disciplina la conservazione separata dei documenti di prova, l'art. 21 cpv. 2 la proroga della protezione in caso di deposito multiplo. La divergenza figura già nel testo originale pubblicato nella RU (RU 2002 1122) in tutte e tre le lingue.
