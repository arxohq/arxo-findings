ARXO-2026-0052 · Reported

FEES: several fees and payment terms are set in "Notes" and footnotes, which are not part of an AIFC Act

Section 26(1)(a) of the AIFC Regulations on AIFC Acts says that "a footnote, endnote or other note" is not part of an AIFC Act, even if inserted by an amending act (section 26(2)). In FEES the application fee for change of control (1 400 USD), the Approved Individual fee (500 USD), the quarterly payment of variable fees and the deferral of some fees to 1 January 2027 appear under "Notes" headings or in footnotes.

Proposed correction

Move these provisions into numbered paragraphs of the Schedules, or state in FEES that the Notes form part of the Rules.
