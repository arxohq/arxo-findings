ARXO-2026-0089 · Reported

COB 5.6.11 names Insurance Intermediaries for the Takaful disclosure, but COB 1.2.3 does not list it among the rules that apply to them

COB 1.2.3 says that COB does not apply to Insurance Intermediaries "with the exception of COB 3, COB 4, COB 8.5 and COB 11". COB 5.6.11(1), inserted with the Takaful amendments in 2018, names "an Insurance Intermediary" among the persons who must explain the differences between Takaful and conventional contracts. When COB 8.5 was added to the list in 1.2.3, 5.6.11 was not. Because 5.6.11 names Insurance Intermediaries expressly, it is best read as applying to them, but 1.2.3 does not say so.

Proposed correction

Please add COB 5.6.11 to the exceptions in COB 1.2.3.
