ARXO-2026-0041 · Reported

FEES Schedule 1, 1.1-1: the exception for Operating a Digital Asset Trading Facility is printed but was never enacted

Amendments No. 14.1 (9 December 2023) inserted 1.1-1 (additional application fee for Digital Assets activities) without an exception; the same instrument put an exception for Operating a Digital Asset Trading Facility only into 6.1-1. Consolidated versions V15 to V18 print the exception in 1.1-1 as well, and we could not find it in any of the published amending instruments Nos. 12 to 17. Whether a Digital Asset Trading Facility applicant owes the additional fee (now 2 800 USD) depends on which text is right; a consolidated version cannot change legal effect (section 19(2) of the AIFC Regulations on AIFC Acts). The draft in Consultation Paper AFSA-PSRD-CSP-2026-0004 amends 1.1-1 again (the fixed amount is replaced by a table) and prints the words "except for the Regulated Activity of Operating a Digital Asset Trading Facility" as existing text, not as an insertion.

Proposed correction

Since the draft amends 1.1-1 in any case, please either enact the exception expressly in the amending instrument, as new text, or remove it, and identify the instrument that supports it for the period since V15.
