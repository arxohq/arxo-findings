ARXO-2026-0973 · Reported

Justice Laws: historical notes differ between the English and French versions in 8 places

In 8 places of the consolidation, the historical note of a section names an amending provision in one language version only, or in a different series: Canadian Commercial Corporation Act, s. 13, and Canada Council for the Arts Act, s. 20: “1976-77, ch. 34, art. 30” only in French; Energy Administration Act, s. 81: “1976-77, ch. 28, art. 49” only in French; Fisheries Improvement Loans Act, s. 5: “1980-81-82-83, ch. 154, art. 2” only in French; Safe Containers Convention Act, s. 8: “1984, ch. 40, art. 79” only in French; Foreign Enlistment Act, s. 13: “R.S., c. 10(2nd Supp.), s. 64” only in English; Perfluorinated Ion-Exchange Membranes Remission Order, s. 2: English “SOR/87-240, s. 1”, French “TR/87-240, art. 1”; Cruiser Remission Order, s. 7: English “SOR/85-113, s. 1”, French “TR/85-113, art. 1”. The consolidation does not show which version is right; this card does not decide it.

Consequence

A reader of one language version is told of an amending provision that the other version does not list. Historical notes are not part of the enactment; no effect on the law.

Proposed correction

The Department of Justice decides how to correct each place in the consolidation or the XML; the evidence gives the wording of each language version.

Français

Lois du Canada : notes historiques différentes dans les versions anglaise et française à 8 endroits

À 8 endroits de la codification, la note historique d’un article mentionne une disposition modificative dans une seule version linguistique ou dans une autre série, par exemple Loi sur la Corporation commerciale canadienne, art. 13 : « 1976-77, ch. 34, art. 30 » seulement dans la note française; Décret de remise sur les yachts de croisière, art. 7 : en anglais « SOR/85-113, s. 1 », en français « TR/85-113, art. 1 ». La codification ne permet pas de dire quelle version est la bonne; cette fiche ne le tranche pas.
