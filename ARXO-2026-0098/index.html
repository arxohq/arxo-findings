ARXO-2026-0098 · Reported

MAR 2.4.5(b) refers to "a Takeover Offer under the Takeover Rules": which Takeover Rules apply?

MAR 2.4.5(b) lifts the closed-period dealing ban for "undertakings to accept, or the acceptance of, a Takeover Offer under the Takeover Rules". The Glossary defines "Takeover Rules" as Rules prescribed by the AFSA under section 88(1)(a) of the Framework Regulations, which says that the AFSA "may prescribe" such Rules. We did not find any Takeover Rules among the acts published on aifc.kz or in the AFSA online rulebook. Chapter 11 of Part 7 of the AIFC Companies Regulations ("Protection of Minorities in Takeovers") is part of the Regulations, not Rules made by the AFSA under section 88(1)(a). If no Takeover Rules have been made, exemption (b) has nothing to apply to, and a Restricted Person would need clearance under MAR 2.4.4.

Proposed correction

Please identify the Takeover Rules that apply for MAR 2.4.5(b), or confirm that this exemption currently has no application. If it is meant to apply now, please consider referring simply to "a Takeover Offer", which the Glossary defines.
