ARXO-2026-0067 · Reported

Glossary: three insurance client money terms point to COB 11.8, which no longer exists

"Client Money Distribution Rules" ("COB 11.8.14"), "Client Money" ("COB 11.8.4") and "Segregated Client" ("COB 11.8") point to COB 11.8. Chapter 11 of the current COB ends at 11.7; the client money rules for insurance intermediation are in COB 8.5 (including 8.5.5, money held as agent of an insurer, and 8.5.15, distribution rules). The draft in Consultation Paper AFSA-PSRD-CSP-2026-0005 changes the fourth such entry, "Insurance Intermediary Audit Report", to refer to GEN 6.3.9, which we support, but leaves these three as they are.

Proposed correction

Please reconcile the three definitions with COB 8.5.
