ARXO-2026-0086 · Reported

COB 8.5 was extended to Insurance Managers but the distribution waterfall and agency rule still name only the Insurance Intermediary

Amendments No. 2 rewrote the old COB 11.8 as COB 8.5 for "an Insurance Intermediary or Insurance Manager", and that phrase runs through COB 8.5. Three places still name only the Insurance Intermediary: 8.5.15(2)(c) "where insufficient funds are held by the Insurance Intermediary"; 8.5.15(2)(d)(i) "trustee in bankruptcy over the Insurance Intermediary, payment must be made accordance with the AIFC Insolvency Regulations"; and 8.5.5(b) "once the money is received by the Insurance Intermediary". The Investment Business rule 8.2.16(2)(d)(i) covers "the Authorised Firm or the Nominee Company".

Proposed correction

Please add "or Insurance Manager" after "Insurance Intermediary" in COB 8.5.15(2)(c), 8.5.15(2)(d)(i) and 8.5.5(b), and read "made in accordance with" in 8.5.15(2)(d)(i).
