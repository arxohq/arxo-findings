ARXO-2026-0977 · Accepted by the addressee

SOR/2024-26 (Canada–Nova Scotia Offshore Area Petroleum Operations Framework Regulations): two French references to paragraphs that do not exist

Subparagraph 31(3)(a)(i): English “the requirements referred to in paragraph 28(1)(b) are met”, French “les conditions prévues à l’alinéa 26(1)b) sont remplies”. Subparagraph 36(1)(a)(ii): English “the requirements referred to in paragraph 28(1)(b) are no longer being met”, French “l’une ou l’autre des conditions prévues à l’alinéa 27(1)b) n’est plus remplie”. Sections 26 and 27 have no subsections and no paragraph (b). The errors are in the regulations as published in the Canada Gazette, Part II, Vol. 158, No. 5 (February 28, 2024), and in the consolidated XML.

Consequence

The reference in one language version leads to no provision, or to a different one than the other version. Both versions are equally authoritative; the effect on how the provision applies was not assessed.

Proposed correction

Replace the French references to 26(1)b) and 27(1)b), in 31(3)(a)(i) and 36(1)(a)(ii), with 28(1)b).
