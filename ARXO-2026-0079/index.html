aifc.kz, The Structure of the Bodies of the AIFC: two links serve the wrong file

The links "… ru (initial)" and "… with amendments as of 9 October 2017. ENG" serve the same file as the 2021 Russian consolidation ("с изменениями и дополнениями по состоянию на 1 июля 2021 года"). The initial Russian text and the 2017 English version are not available.
