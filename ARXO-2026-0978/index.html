ARXO-2026-0978 · Reported

Certain Russian Goods Remission Order (SOR/2023-160), s. 2(2), English: reference to paragraph (1)(b), which does not exist

Subsection 2(2) reads “in the case of goods referred to in paragraph (1)(b) that were imported before the day on which this subsection comes into force”. Subsection 2(1) has no paragraph (b). The goods meant are in paragraph 1(b), tariff item No. 8807.20.00; the French version reads “s’agissant de marchandises visées à l’alinéa 1b)”. Subsection 2(2) was added by SOR/2026-140 (Canada Gazette, Part II, Vol. 160, No. 13, July 1, 2026), and the error is in the English text as published there.

Consequence

The reference in one language version leads to no provision, or to a different one than the other version. Both versions are equally authoritative; the effect on how the provision applies was not assessed.

Proposed correction

Amend the English version of subsection 2(2) by replacing “paragraph (1)(b)” with “paragraph 1(b)”.

Français

Décret de remise visant certaines marchandises de la Russie (DORS/2023-160), par. 2(2), version anglaise : renvoi à l’alinéa (1)b), qui n’existe pas

La version anglaise du paragraphe 2(2) se lit « in the case of goods referred to in paragraph (1)(b) that were imported before the day on which this subsection comes into force ». Le paragraphe 2(1) n’a pas d’alinéa b). Les marchandises visées figurent à l’alinéa 1b) (nº tarifaire 8807.20.00), comme le dit la version française : « s’agissant de marchandises visées à l’alinéa 1b) ». Le paragraphe 2(2) a été ajouté par le DORS/2026-140, Gazette du Canada, Partie II, vol. 160, nº 13 (1er juillet 2026), et l’erreur figure dans le texte anglais publié.
