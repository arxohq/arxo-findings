ARXO-2026-0096 · Reported

MAR 1.9.4(4)(a): the Expert limb of the correction defence refers to "(2)(c)", which is not about Expert statements; the Expert paragraph is (3)

MAR 1.9.4(4)(a) gives a defence where, before acquisition, "a correction or, where the statement was such as is mentioned in (2)(c), the fact that the Expert was not competent or had not consented" was published. Paragraph (2)(c) is the general correction defence and describes no statement; statements made on the authority of an Expert are dealt with in (3). Read literally, the second limb of (4)(a) has no case to apply to. The same reference is already in Amendments No. 7 (29 June 2022), which inserted 1.9.3 and 1.9.4: the instrument lettered the top-level paragraphs (a) to (f) but its own cross-references already used the numbers (2) to (6), so "(2)(c)" pointed to the correction defence from the start. The DFSA and ADGM market rules from which this text comes have the same reference (DFSA MKT 2.11.1(4)(a), ADGM MKT 4.11.1(4)(a)). MAR 1.9.3 and 1.9.4 appear in the consolidated MAR (V13) on aifc.kz but not in the AFSA online rulebook (see AIFC2-MKT-13).

Proposed correction

Please replace "mentioned in (2)(c)" with "mentioned in (3)" in MAR 1.9.4(4)(a).
