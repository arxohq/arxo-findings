ARXO-2026-0087 · Reported

Amendments No. 14 apply COB 8.2 to Money Service Providers but 'Client Money' (8.2.1, Glossary) still covers only Investment Business money

Since Amendments No. 14 (13 January 2026), COB 8.1.2 and 8.1.4 require an Authorised Firm that Provides Money Services to comply with COB 8.2, and PMS 9.1 sends a Money Service Provider to a Client Money Account "as prescribed under COB 8". The intended scheme follows from these rules. But COB 8.2.1, the heading of COB 8.2 ("Client Money: Investment Business") and the Glossary definition of "Client Money" still describe only Money held in connection with Investment Business, so the definition and the heading do not match the rule that applies them.

Proposed correction

Please align the Client Money definition (COB 8.2.1 and the Glossary) and the heading of COB 8.2 with COB 8.1.4 and PMS 9.
