ARXO-2026-0166 · Reported

AUD 9.2.1 table: the rows for Non-Exempt Funds and Reporting Entities refer to CIR 11.4(a) and MAR 3.2.2, which do not require an audit

The table in AUD 9.2.1 links each auditor's report to the rule that requires it. Two rows do not lead there: "Financial Statements - Non-Exempt Fund" refers to "CIR 11.4(a)", a rulebook name the Glossary no longer has (the fund auditor rule is CIS 10.4), and "Financial Statements - Reporting Entity" refers to MAR 3.2.2, which is "Signing of the annual report" (the audit duty is MAR 3.1.2). The rows for the client asset reports refer to COB 8.2.19, 8.3.14 and 11.8.15, which no longer require an auditor's report; the draft in Consultation Paper AFSA-PSRD-CSP-2026-0005 replaces these references with a new GEN 6.3.9, and we support that change, but it leaves the two rows above as they are. In the draft GEN 6.3.9, item (a)(iv) requires an "Insurance Intermediary Auditor's Report" "if the Authorised Firm is permitted to control or hold Client Money". This is the same condition as for the Client Money Auditor's Report in (a)(ii) and is not limited to insurance intermediaries; the Glossary and the AUD table call this report "Insurance Intermediary Audit Report".

Proposed correction

Please update the two rows to the provisions that require the audit (for example CIS 10.4 and MAR 3.1.2). When finalising the draft GEN 6.3.9, please limit item (a)(iv) to insurance intermediaries that hold or control Client Money and use one name for the report.
