ARXO-2026-0644 · Reported

Gloas: the builder onboarding note describes a deposit window that is empty unless the deposit queue has a backlog

The honest builder guide says a builder can be onboarded at the fork with a deposit made late enough to be still pending at the fork, but early enough that its slot is finalized, so that the builder is active. Without a backlog in the pending deposit queue no slot meets both conditions: Fulu applies every pending deposit up to the finalized slot, and a builder is active only if its deposit epoch is before the finalized epoch. A deposit that is early enough is applied by Fulu as a validator with a 0xB0 credential, which is not an execution withdrawal credential, so the validator never gets withdrawals. A deposit that is late enough becomes a builder that is not active at the fork. The activation note of the same guide is correct as a condition.

Consequence

Minimal preset, fork at epoch 6, finalized epoch 4 at the upgrade: a deposit in slot 31 becomes validator 64 with a 0xB0 credential and a balance of 32 ETH, and no builder; a deposit in slot 33 becomes builder 0, which is not active at the fork.

Proposed correction

Tell builders to deposit in the last epoch before the fork, so that the deposit is still pending at the fork and the builder becomes active about two epochs later, and warn that a deposit applied before the fork creates a validator that cannot withdraw. Making builders active from the fork slot would need a change of the rule, which is a design choice.
