ARXO-2026-0494 · Reported

Environmental Protection Agency: 54 references to 40 CFR parts 1500 to 1508, removed in 2025

In the CFR chapters assigned to the Environmental Protection Agency, 54 references in 19 sections cite 40 CFR parts 1500 to 1508 or sections in those parts. CEQ removed and reserved these parts effective April 11, 2025 (90 FR 10610). The eCFR of October 27, 2026 shows PARTS 1500-1508 [RESERVED].

Consequence

A reader who follows these references finds no text at the cited address. No effect on a decision or an amount has been established.

Proposed correction

The agency decides whether to remove or replace each reference. The removal rule does not name a replacement part.
