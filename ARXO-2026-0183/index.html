ARXO-2026-0183 · Reported

Insolvency Regulations s.88(2): the Rules may vary "requirements mentioned in subsection (2)", i.e. in the power itself; the criteria are in (1)

Section 88(1) says the Rules must prescribe the criteria for registration as an insolvency practitioner or official liquidator. Section 88(2) lets the Rules vary "requirements mentioned in subsection (2)" for applicants regulated outside the AIFC. Subsection (2) is the power itself and mentions no requirements; the criteria are in (1). The parallel provision for auditors, section 134(3) of the Companies Regulations, refers to the subsection that sets the criteria.

Proposed correction

Please replace "subsection (2)" with "subsection (1)" in section 88(2).
