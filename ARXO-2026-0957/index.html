ARXO-2026-0957 · Accepted by the addressee

SR footnotes: 37 dates with a misspelt month or a month abbreviation not in the SR form

In 37 footnotes of 19 acts of the Classified Compilation (SR), a date is printed with a misspelt month or with a month abbreviation that differs from the form used in the SR, for example «1. Augst 2008» or «15 octobtre 2018». The dates remain readable. Each finding was read in the PDF of the consolidated version, which is the authoritative form.

Consequence

The dates remain readable; the misspellings make them harder to search and to read by machine. No effect on the law.

Proposed correction

The Federal Chancellery decides how to correct each place; the evidence gives the wording of each language version.
