Arxo Findings Register

Verified discrepancies in legal texts, official publications and implementations, with evidence and correction histories.

Disclosure rule. A finding enters the register after Arxo has confirmed it and has reported it to every institution responsible for the text, publication or program concerned. The register does not wait for a reply. Replies, corrections and changes to our own conclusion are added to the record; records are never deleted or renumbered. Findings that were corrected before our report are marked as such. Findings reported before the register opened carry their original dates. Principles · How findings are made.
