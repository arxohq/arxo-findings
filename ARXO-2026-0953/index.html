ARXO-2026-0953 · Accepted by the addressee

SR footnotes: in 4 articles one language version cites the AS entry of a different act

In 4 articles of 3 acts of the Classified Compilation (SR), the footnotes of all language versions describe the same amending act, but in one version the reference to the Official Compilation (AS) is that of a different act. Each finding was read in the PDF of the consolidated version, which is the authoritative form. Which language version is right is not decided here.

Consequence

A reader who follows the footnote of one language version reaches a different entry of the Official Compilation than readers of the other versions, or none. No effect on the content of the provisions has been established.

Proposed correction

The Federal Chancellery decides how to correct each place; the evidence gives the wording of each language version.

Deutsch

SR-Fussnoten: In 4 Artikeln verweist eine Sprachfassung auf die AS-Fundstelle eines anderen Erlasses

In 4 Artikeln aus 3 Erlassen der Systematischen Rechtssammlung (SR) beschreiben die Fussnoten aller Sprachfassungen denselben Änderungserlass; in einer Fassung ist die Fundstelle der Amtlichen Sammlung (AS) jedoch die eines anderen Erlasses. Jeder Befund wurde im PDF der konsolidierten Fassung gelesen, das massgebend ist. Welche Sprachfassung zutrifft, legen wir nicht fest.

Ogni constatazione è stata letta nel PDF della versione consolidata, che è determinante. Non stabiliamo quale versione linguistica sia corretta.
