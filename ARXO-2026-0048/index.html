ARXO-2026-0048 · Reported

Fees Rules V15: cover date "as of 9 December 2022" is that of V14; two instruments numbered 14.1

The cover of V15 and its label on aifc.kz read "with amendments as of 9 December 2022", the date already used for V14. The instruments consolidated in V15 are dated 10 September 2023, 10 September 2023 and 9 December 2023, and two of them are both numbered "Amendments No. 14.1".

Proposed correction

Please correct the V15 date to 9 December 2023 and distinguish the two instruments labelled No. 14.1 by their approval dates.
