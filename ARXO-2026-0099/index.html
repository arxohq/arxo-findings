ARXO-2026-0099 · Reported

MAR Schedule 4 para 4(a)(iii) points to 'technology and governance requirements set out in AMI 2-1.2'; those are in AMI 2-1.1

MAR Schedule 4 para 4(a)(iii) requires a Security Token prospectus to explain 'how the operator of the facility meets the technology and governance requirements set out in AMI 2-1.2'. In AMI V11 the technology and governance requirements are AMI 2-1.1 ('Technology and governance requirements'); AMI 2-1.2 is 'Operating a facility for Investment Tokens that permits direct access'. The same mismatch existed when Schedule 4 was introduced (AMI V8, 1 Jan 2024).

Proposed correction

Please replace "AMI 2-1.2" with "AMI 2-1.1" in MAR Schedule 4, paragraph 4(a)(iii). The words "technology and governance requirements" already fix the meaning, so this is an editorial correction under section 19(3)(a) of the AIFC Regulations on AIFC Acts.
