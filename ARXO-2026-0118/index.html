ARXO-2026-0118 · Reported

IFR 7.3 requires SSB approval of a Fund's "Prospectus", a Glossary term for securities prospectuses; Funds issue "Offering Materials"

IFR 7.3(1) and (2) require a Fund’s Constitution "and Prospectus" to be approved by an SSB and reviewed every year; the Guidance under 7.2 also speaks of the Fund’s "most recent Prospectus". In the Glossary, "Prospectus" is the document referred to in MAR 1.3, which an unlisted Fund does not issue; such a Fund gives investors "Offering Materials" (CIS 5.3), and IFR itself uses that term in 7.8. Read with 7.8, "Prospectus" in 7.3 can be understood as the Fund’s Offering Materials, but the defined term points elsewhere. CIS has moved to the same term for Funds: Amendments No. 7 to CIS (in force 1 January 2025) replaced "Prospectus" with "Offering Materials" in the Feeder Fund rules. IFR 7.3 was not aligned.

Proposed correction

Please use "Offering Materials" in IFR 7.3(1), 7.3(2) and the Guidance under 7.2, or state that "Prospectus" there includes a Fund’s Offering Materials.
