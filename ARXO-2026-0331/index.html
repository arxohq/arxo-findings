ARXO-2026-0331 · Reported

raltegravir: wording question in 2.1

2.1 reads “Maximum daily dose is 300 mg taken by mouth twice daily”. Example: "Maximum daily dose" can be read as 300 mg per day or as 600 mg per day. The same text is in 7 labels from 6 labelers in the DailyMed release of 29 October 2026.

Consequence

Potential effect on a prescribing decision: low; careful reading of the whole label usually resolves it. Arxo does not say which instruction is correct and gives no dose.
