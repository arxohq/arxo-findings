Schedule 8 (2025) e-text: line 75 has no percent sign

In the accessible e-text of Schedule 8 for 2025, Part 5, line 75 says "multiply by 83.1933 =". The PDF of the same form, the read-aloud text of the PDF and line 9 of the e-text all have "83.1933%". Read as printed, line 75 would be 100 times too large. It cancels out by line 88, so the final amounts do not change. In the same e-text, page 1 says "Page 1 of 10" (the other pages say "of 11"), and the opening instruction on page 6 is labelled "Line 36", where the PDF has line 37.

Consequence

No final amount changes. A reader who works through the e-text by hand or with a screen reader gets different intermediate lines: with an amount of 100.00, line 75 is 8,319.33 in the e-text and 83.19 in the PDF.

Proposed correction

Line 75: "multiplied by 83.1933% =", as on line 9. Page 1: "Page 1 of 11". Page 6: "Line 37".
