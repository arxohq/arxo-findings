ARXO-2026-0399 · Reported

Basel Framework: RBC25.9 FAQ3

RBC25.9 FAQ3 says an FX option hedging a banking book position is covered by RBC25.9(6), which only lists options on credit or equity risk from the bank's own issued instruments.

Consequence

Position: Bought EUR/USD option, notional 50, hedging FX risk of a banking-book SME loan. Reading a: FAQ3: presumptive trading book unless supervisor approves; RBC25.8(8) overridden. Reading b: RBC25.9(6) does not apply; RBC25.8(8) applies, banking book. Difference: Market risk capital on notional 50 vs none.

Proposed correction

Clarify or correct the cited passage so that the rule and its references give one result for the stated case.
