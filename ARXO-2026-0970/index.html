ARXO-2026-0970 · Reported

Justice Laws: repeated subparagraph labels in 8 places of the consolidated XML

In 8 places of the consolidated Acts and regulations, a paragraph has two subparagraphs with the same label in one language version: Financial Institutions Depositors Compensation Act, 5(2)(b), English; Income Tax Act, 142.6(8)(b), French; Electronic Payments Regulations, 3(b), English; Transportation of Dangerous Goods Regulations, 5.10(1)(d), French; Proceeds of Crime (Money Laundering) and Terrorist Financing Regulations, 89(e), French; Canada Offshore Renewable Energy Regulations, 40(3)(e), French; Defence Services Pension Part V Regulations, 34(4)(b), French; Determination of Country of Origin for the Purpose of Marking Goods (CUSMA Countries) Regulations, 2(2)(b), French. The origin has not been established: the enacted or registered texts were not checked.

Consequence

A reference to the repeated label does not identify one subparagraph in that language version. No effect on how a provision applies has been established.

Proposed correction

Where the difference comes from the enacted or registered text, it needs an amendment by the responsible department; otherwise the consolidation can be corrected.
