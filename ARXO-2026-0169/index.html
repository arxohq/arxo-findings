ARXO-2026-0169 · Reported

Bank-cooperation Rules: Schedule 2 still shows the 2023 DAA capital table (Self-managed Fund row, old DATF measure), while 3.3.1 refers to the DAA rules

Rule 3.3.1 of the Rules and mechanisms of cooperation with second-tier banks requires Digital Asset Service Providers to hold minimum capital "in accordance with rules 2.2. and 3.2" of the DAA, and 3.3.2 says the amounts "are specified in Schedule 2". Schedule 2, both in the PDF on aifc.kz (approved 11 December 2023) and on the regulator's website, still has (1) the row "Managing a Collective Investment Scheme, which is a Self-managed Fund ..." with USD 200,000, which the DAA no longer contains (it is absent from the consolidated version in force from 1 January 2025), and (2) the old measure for operating a Digital Asset Trading Facility: "sufficient working capital ... in different market conditions (both negative and positive scenarios)". Since 1 January 2026 the DAA uses "operational expenses in fiat currency". The other rows match. It is not clear which source governs.

Proposed correction

Please confirm whether Schedule 2 is meant to set its own amounts or only to reproduce the DAA, and then update Schedule 2 to the current DAA tables or replace it with a reference to DAA 2.2 and 3.2.
