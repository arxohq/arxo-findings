ARXO-2026-0084 · Reported

COB 1.2.4 lists 'COB 15 (Record Keeping)' for Trust Service Providers: COB 15 is complaints, record keeping is COB 16

COB 1.2.4 disapplies COB to Trust Service Providers except, among others, "COB 15 (Record Keeping)". Chapter 15 is "Complaints Handling and Dispute Resolution"; Chapter 16 is "Record Keeping and Internal Audit"; and section 15.4 inside Chapter 15 is "Record keeping" for complaints. The number and the label point to different provisions. The mismatch exists from the first version of COB (2017) and was kept through every later change to 1.2.4. Other exclusion rules name the chapters correctly, for example 1.2.2(g): "COB 15 (Complaints Handling and Dispute Resolution), COB 16 (Record Keeping and Internal Audit)".

Proposed correction

Please state which rules apply to Trust Service Providers: (1) COB 15 (Complaints Handling and Dispute Resolution); (2) COB 16 (Record Keeping and Internal Audit); (3) both; or (4) COB 15.4 (Record keeping), the complaint-records section of COB 15.
