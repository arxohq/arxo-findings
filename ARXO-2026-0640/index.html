ARXO-2026-0640 · Reported

Gloas: the payload availability bit of the fork slot is never cleared

The Gloas upgrade sets every bit of the payload availability vector to 1. After that, the processing of each slot clears the bit of the next slot. The bit of the fork slot would be cleared while the last Fulu slot is processed, but that slot runs under the Fulu rules, before the upgrade, so the bit stays 1 even when the payload of the fork block is never revealed. Attestation rewards read this bit to decide whether a vote matches the payload status. The case differs from issue 5661, where the last Fulu slot is empty and the bit belongs to a Fulu slot.

Consequence

Minimal preset, fork at slot 16: block 16 is applied and its payload is withheld, slot 17 is empty, and block 18 builds on block 16 without its payload. Honest attesters of slot 17 who saw no payload vote index 0 and get the timely head flag in 0 of 4 cases; attesters who vote index 1 get it in 4 of 4. The same shape at slot 20 gives the reverse result, 4 of 4 for index 0.

Proposed correction

Clear the bit of the fork slot in the Gloas upgrade function. The bits of the Fulu slots stay 1, because Fulu blocks carry their payload. With this one-line change the fork slot gives the same result as slot 20.
