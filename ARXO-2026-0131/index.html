ARXO-2026-0131 · Reported

IBB NSFR rule cites 'Section E of Appendix 1' (does not exist) and then 'Section B' in the same paragraph

IBB 10.28(2) first says that the NSFR methodology is "set out in Section E of Appendix 1 of the IBB Module", and then refers to "Section B of Appendix 1" for the ASF and RSF factors. Appendix 1 has only Sections A (LCR), B (NSFR) and C (Maturity Mismatch). The meaning survives through the second sentence.

Proposed correction

Please read "Section B" for "Section E" in the first sentence of IBB 10.28(2) (an editorial correction under section 19(3)(a) of the AIFC Regulations on AIFC Acts).
