ARXO-2026-0334 · Reported

CSS Cascade 6: §3.6 on presentational hints points to Level 5 §6.4 Cascade Layers instead of §6.5

§3.6 has no text of its own, only an issue box pointing to “CSS Cascading 5 § 6.4 Cascade Layers”. In Level 5 presentational hints are §6.5, and Level 5 moved them out of layers into their own origin (issue 6659). The other pointers of the same kind in Level 6 go to the matching Level 5 section; the Working Draft of 6 September 2024 has the same pointer in §2.6.

Consequence

A reader who follows the pointer lands on Cascade Layers, which no longer govern presentational hints.

Proposed correction

Point §3.6 to Level 5 §6.5 Precedence of Non-CSS Presentational Hints.
