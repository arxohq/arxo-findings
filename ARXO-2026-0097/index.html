ARXO-2026-0097 · Reported

MAR 5.5.3: the closing words "prior to conducting a Market Sounding" also cover the record of disclosures in (c), which can only be completed after the sounding

MAR 5.5.3 lists four steps (a) to (d) and then, on a separate line after (d), the words "prior to conducting a Market Sounding". Laid out this way, the words govern the whole list. Steps (a) and (b) already carry their own timing. Step (c) requires a record of the disclosures, including "the date and time of each disclosure", which cannot be complete before the sounding. The AFSA online text joins the closing words to (d) instead. The second sentence of (c) also asks for the record to be given to "the competent authority", while (d) names the AFSA.

Proposed correction

Please make clear that the closing words apply to (a) only, or delete them, since (a) and (b) already set the timing; and use "the AFSA" in (c), or delete the second sentence of (c) as a repeat of (d).
