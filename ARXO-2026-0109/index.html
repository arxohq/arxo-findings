ARXO-2026-0109 · Reported

CIS 5.1 disapplies 5.3(b)(i) (investment objective, strategy, leverage) for Non-Exempt Funds; in the draft, 5.1 refers to the deleted rule 5.3

CIS 5.1(a) applies 5.3 to all Funds "excluding 5.3(b)(i) and (j)", and 5.1(b) applies "5.3(b)(i) and (j)" to Exempt Funds only. 5.3(b)(i) is the core disclosure of the investment objective, policy and strategy, asset types, risks and leverage, including the maximum level of leverage. The items that are Exempt-only by nature are 5.3(i) (the Exempt Fund legend) and 5.3(j) (the legend for an Exempt Fund of a Foreign Fund Manager). Read literally, a Non-Exempt (retail) Fund is relieved of 5.3(b)(i) while Exempt Funds must give it. The opening words of 5.3(b) and Schedule 1(j) still bind every Fund, so what is literally lost is the specific list, including the maximum level of leverage. The draft in Consultation Paper AFSA-PSRD-CSP-2026-0001 deletes rule 5.3 and moves its content to the new 5.4, 5.5 and Schedule 1-1, while 5.1(a) and (b) still refer to "5.3 (excluding 5.3(b)(i) and (j))" and "5.3(b)(i) and (j)". If the draft is adopted as published, both references will point to a deleted rule.

Proposed correction

Please confirm whether 5.1(a) and (b) should refer to the Exempt Fund legends (5.3(i) and (j)) rather than to 5.3(b)(i). When finalising the draft, please redirect 5.1(a) and (b) to the new content rules (5.4 and 5.5) and name the items that apply to Exempt Funds only.
