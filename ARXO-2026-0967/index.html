ARXO-2026-0967 · Reported

SR 642.211 (VStV), Art. 41b: the German and Italian versions refer to Art. 6 para. 2 VStG, the French version to Art. 6 para. 1 VStG

Art. 41b of the Withholding Tax Ordinance (VStV) refers to Art. 6 para. 2 of the Withholding Tax Act (VStG) in the German and Italian versions and to Art. 6 para. 1 in the French version. Both paragraphs exist: para. 1 concerns winnings from gambling games, para. 2 winnings from lotteries and games of skill for sales promotion; Art. 41b itself speaks of the latter in all three versions. The divergence is already in the amendment of April 10, 2024 (AS 2024 161), in force since June 1, 2024.

Consequence

Readers of different language versions are sent to different provisions; at most one of them can be the one intended. No effect on a decision has been established.

Proposed correction

The authors of the act decide which reference is intended; the evidence gives the wording of each language version.

Deutsch

SR 642.211 (VStV), Art. 41b: deutsch und italienisch Verweis auf Art. 6 Abs. 2 VStG, französisch auf Art. 6 Abs. 1 VStG

Die deutsche und die italienische Fassung von Art. 41b VStV verweisen auf Art. 6 Abs. 2 VStG, die französische auf Art. 6 Abs. 1 VStG. Beide Absätze bestehen: Art. 6 Abs. 1 VStG betrifft Gewinne aus Geldspielen, Abs. 2 Gewinne aus Lotterien und Geschicklichkeitsspielen zur Verkaufsförderung. Die Abweichung steht bereits in der Änderung vom 10. April 2024 (AS 2024 161), in Kraft seit 1. Juni 2024.

Français

RS 642.211 (OIA), art. 41b : les versions allemande et italienne renvoient à l'art. 6, al. 2, LIA, la version française à l'art. 6, al. 1, LIA

L'art. 41b de l'ordonnance sur l'impôt anticipé renvoie à l'art. 6, al. 2, LIA dans les versions allemande et italienne et à l'art. 6, al. 1, LIA dans la version française. Les deux alinéas existent : l'al. 1 vise les gains provenant de jeux d'argent, l'al. 2 les gains provenant de jeux d'adresse ou de loteries destinés à promouvoir les ventes. La divergence figure déjà dans la modification du 10 avril 2024 (RO 2024 161), en vigueur depuis le 1er juin 2024.

it

RS 642.211 (OIPrev), art. 41b: le versioni tedesca e italiana rinviano all'art. 6 cpv. 2 LIP, la versione francese all'art. 6 cpv. 1 LIP

L'art. 41b dell'ordinanza sull'imposta preventiva rinvia all'art. 6 cpv. 2 LIP nelle versioni tedesca e italiana e all'art. 6 cpv. 1 LIP nella versione francese. Entrambi i capoversi esistono: il cpv. 1 riguarda le vincite ai giochi in denaro, il cpv. 2 le vincite ai giochi di destrezza e alle lotterie destinati a promuovere le vendite. La divergenza figura già nella modifica del 10 aprile 2024 (RU 2024 161), in vigore dal 1° giugno 2024.
