ARXO-2026-0039 · Reported

FEES 1.3 refers to Schedule 6 for Approved Individual application fees; Schedule 6 has none

FEES 1.3 says Approved Individual fees are "as specified in Schedules 1, 2 or 6". Schedule 6 sets annual supervision fees only. Approved Individual fees are in Schedules 1 and 2, in Schedule 3 (change of MLRO, Audit Principal) and in Schedule 8 (FinTech Lab, 200 USD). Amendments No. 12 replaced "3 or 4" with "or 6" in 2022.

Proposed correction

Please replace the reference with the provisions that actually set individual-approval fees (Schedules 1 and 2, and as applicable Schedules 3 and 8).
