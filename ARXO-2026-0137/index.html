ARXO-2026-0137 · Reported

Glossary still defines 'Captive Insurer' as group-only, contrary to PINS 14.1.1 (Class 2 and 3 since 2020); FEES fee rows depend on it

Glossary V23: Captive Insurer = Licence to carry on Insurance Business 'only for the business or operations of the Group to which it belongs'. PINS 14.1.1-14.1.2 (since 13.12.2020): Class 2 may take up to 20% of GWP from third-party risks; Class 3 insures persons in similar businesses (group captives). The PINS 'Working Schedule 1' list of defined terms also keeps the group-only text. FEES V18 charges 'Captive Insurance Business' USD 7,000 (application) / 4,200 (annual) versus 'Insurance Business' 14,000 / 9,800.

Proposed correction

Please define "Captive Insurer" and "Captive Insurance Business" in the Glossary by reference to PINS 14.1.1 and 14.1.2, and update PINS Working Schedule 1.
