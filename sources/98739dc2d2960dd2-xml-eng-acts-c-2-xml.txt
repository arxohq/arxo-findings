Canada Council for the Arts Act — s. 20 (English)
Audit20The accounts and financial transactions of the Council shall be audited annually by the Auditor General of Canada and a report of the audit shall be made to the Council and to the member of the Queen’s Privy Council for Canada designated under subsection 21(1). R.S., c. C-2, s. 22
