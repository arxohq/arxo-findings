Excise Duty Indexing Ratio Regulations — s. 3 (English)
3For the purpose of adjusting the duties of excise enumerated in Part IV of the schedule to the Excise Act, the ratio determined according to clause 2(b)(i)(B) of Part V of that schedule is altered by multiplying the ratio(a)by 1.37078 for the adjustment on the first day of September, 1983; and(b)by 1.88464 for the adjustment on the first day of September, 1984.
