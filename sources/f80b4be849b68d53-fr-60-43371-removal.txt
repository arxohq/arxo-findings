Part 1614 replaced the complaint process located at 29 CFR part 1613 (‘‘part 1613’’), after October 1, 1992. Thus, part 1613 is obsolete. Accordingly, this regulation removes 29 CFR part 1613 and amends 29 CFR part 1614 to delete references to part 1613.

EFFECTIVE DATE: August 21, 1995.

1. Part 1613 is removed.
