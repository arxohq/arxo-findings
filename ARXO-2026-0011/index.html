ARXO-2026-0011 · Accepted by the addressee

eCFR shows $84,852 in 11 CFR 111.24(a)(2)(ii); the January 2025 rule replaced it with $87,056

Published at a date promised in advance to the press (2026-10-28T11:00:00-04:00); the responsible institutions had been told earlier, as listed below.

The Federal Election Commission's 2025 inflation adjustment (90 FR 210, FR Doc. 2024-31368) amends section 111.24 by replacing the amount in paragraph (a)(2)(ii) with $87,056. The eCFR still shows the earlier $84,852 in its versions as of 31 December 2025 and 20 October 2026: the amendment to this paragraph was not incorporated.

Consequence

Anyone reading the eCFR sees a maximum penalty for a knowing and willful violation under paragraph (a)(2)(ii) that is $2,204 lower than the rule sets. No penalty actually assessed on the eCFR figure is claimed.

Proposed correction

$87,056, as set by the rule.
