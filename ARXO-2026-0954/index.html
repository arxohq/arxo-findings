ARXO-2026-0954 · Accepted by the addressee

SR footnotes: in 42 articles the language versions cite different AS entries

In 42 articles of 27 acts of the Classified Compilation (SR), the footnotes on the pages of the article cite different entries of the Official Compilation (AS) in the German, French and Italian versions. Each finding was read in the PDF of the consolidated version, which is the authoritative form. Which language version is right is not decided here.

Consequence

A reader who follows the footnote of one language version reaches a different entry of the Official Compilation than readers of the other versions, or none. No effect on the content of the provisions has been established.

Proposed correction

The Federal Chancellery decides how to correct each place; the evidence gives the wording of each language version.
