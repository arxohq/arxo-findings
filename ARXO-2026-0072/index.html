ARXO-2026-0072 · Reported

PRU (Investment Firms) 3.2: the definition of Capital Resources refers three times to "the Insurance Intermediary"

Rule 3.2(1)(a) counts the share capital "of the PRU Investment Firm", but 3.2(1)(c) counts retained earnings and reserves "of the Insurance Intermediary", and the deductions in 3.2(2)(b)(iv) and (v) refer to the pension fund assets and own shares of "the Insurance Intermediary". The intended meaning is clear from context, but the text names a different kind of firm. The Prudential Rules for Insurance Intermediaries now say "the Authorised Firm" in the same items.

Proposed correction

Please replace the three references to "the Insurance Intermediary" in PRU INV 3.2 with "the PRU Investment Firm".
