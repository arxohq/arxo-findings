ARXO-2026-0217 · Reported

aifc.kz page of the Regulations on Damages and Remedies sends readers for the 'current version' to the AFSA Auditor Rules

Under "Effective Version" the aifc.kz page of the AIFC Regulations on Damages and Remedies states "current version can be found on Regulator's website:" and links https://orderly.myafsa.com/articles/auditorrules. That section of the AFSA rulebook is the AIFC Auditor Rules. The Regulations on Damages and Remedies (No. 17 of 2017) are not an AFSA rulebook. The same link appears on the aifc.kz page of the Auditor Rules. The only PDF offered is the 2017 text.

Proposed correction

Please remove the AFSA link from the page of the Regulations on Damages and Remedies, or replace it with the location of the current text of these Regulations.
