ARXO-2026-0968 · Reported

Security of Canada Information Disclosure Act, s. 2(1), definition “activity that undermines the security of Canada”, paragraph (g): amendment not applied in the French version

The English version refers to “section 2 of the Communications Security Establishment Act”. The French version still reads “au sens de l’article 273.61 de la Loi sur la défense nationale”. Section 89 of S.C. 2019, c. 13 replaced paragraph (g) in both languages; it came into force on August 1, 2019 under SI/2019-70, after subsection 115(3) of the same Act, which came into force on June 21, 2019. The consolidated French version does not show the replacement.

Consequence

A reader of the French consolidated version reads a reference to a provision of the National Defence Act that the amending Act replaced. The enacted text, which prevails, is not affected. No effect on a decision has been established.

Proposed correction

Bring the French consolidated version into line with section 89 of S.C. 2019, c. 13.
