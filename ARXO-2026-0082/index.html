ARXO-2026-0082 · Reported

COB 15.5.4 points to 'paragraph 2 of COB 15.4.2', a one-paragraph rule on the content of complaint records

COB 15.5.4 requires information about other means of resolving complaints, "as outlined in paragraph 2 of COB 15.4.2", to be made available on the website, at branches and in the client terms of a Money Service Provider. COB 15.4.2 ("Content of records") has a single paragraph on what a complaints record must contain; it has no paragraph 2. The duty to inform a complainant of other means of resolving the Complaint is the second paragraph of COB 15.2.5. The wrong number is already in Amendments No. 14, which inserted COB 15.5.4. The descriptive words keep the meaning.

Proposed correction

Please confirm that the reference in COB 15.5.4 should be to the second paragraph of COB 15.2.5, and correct it. As the descriptive words already fix the meaning, this can be an editorial correction under section 19(3)(a) of the AIFC Regulations on AIFC Acts.
