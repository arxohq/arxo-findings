ARXO-2026-0107 · Reported

CIS 5.3(l) is headed 'Information relating to a Feeder Fund' but sets property-fund disclosures; 'Property Fund' is not defined

Paragraph (l) of CIS 5.3 opens 'Information relating to a Feeder Fund:' and then lists only real-estate items: investment in Real Property, the 'Property Fund's' net assets in property-related assets, vacant or mortgaged properties, valuer under 6.10(b), insurance of Real Property, single-property funds, REIT vehicle. Nothing in (l) concerns Feeder Funds (whose disclosures sit in 2.4-1(c)(v)). The term 'Property Fund' used in (l)(ii), (iv), (v), (vi)(E), (vii) and Schedule 1 (j)(i) is not defined in CIS or in Glossary V23. Under s.24 of the AIFC Regulations on AIFC Acts the heading is part of the act, so the text names one scope (Feeder Funds) and the content implies another (funds investing in Real Property, incl. REITs).

Proposed correction

Please replace the heading of CIS 5.3(l) with "Information relating to a Property Fund" (or "a Fund investing in Real Property") and define the term in the Glossary; the scope is for the AFSA to set.
