ARXO-2026-0620 · Reported

PDOC: when a new edition starts, time zones east of Eastern time get the amounts of the previous day

PDOC says that the date the employee is paid decides the tax rates and the CPP and EI figures, and that the July 2026 changes apply to a date paid of July 1, 2026 or later. On 23 October 2026 we ran the public calculator many times with the same entries and changed only the time zone of the browser (automated Google Chrome 155). On the day each new edition we tried took effect (1 July 2025, 1 January 2026 and 1 July 2026) the result screen showed the selected date, but in Atlantic (Halifax) and Newfoundland (St. John's) time PDOC used the amounts of the previous day. Labrador time (Goose Bay), tried on 1 July 2026, behaved the same. Toronto and Vancouver time got the new amounts. The days before and after gave the same result in every time zone. Examples, monthly pay of 4,000.00 CAD: British Columbia, 1 July 2026: provincial tax 132.21 CAD instead of 160.43 CAD. Ontario, 1 January 2026, TD1 amounts as PDOC fills them in: PDOC filled in the 2025 amounts (16,129 and 12,747 instead of 16,452 and 12,989) and showed federal tax 314.59 instead of 310.53, provincial tax 171.40 instead of 170.40 and EI 65.60 instead of 65.20. Newfoundland and Labrador, 1 July 2026: PDOC filled in 11,188 instead of 15,000 as the provincial TD1 amount (provincial tax 255.95 instead of 228.31). The PDF of the result made in Halifax time shows a date one day earlier than the screen. The form sends the date as the local midnight of the computer; we cannot see the server, so we do not know the cause. We did not check what employers actually withheld.

Consequence

A payroll operator in these time zones who enters a pay date of 1 January or 1 July sees the amounts of the previous day under the selected date. Example: provincial tax of 132.21 CAD instead of 160.43 CAD for a monthly pay of 4,000.00 CAD in British Columbia on 1 July 2026.

Proposed correction

One selected date should give one set of rates and amounts, the same pre-filled TD1 amounts and the same date in the PDF, whatever the time zone of the computer.
