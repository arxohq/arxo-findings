ARXO-2026-0021 · Corrected

CGI art. 1600 on Légifrance, version for 2027: the 2026 Finance Act insertion on the Corsica chamber of commerce is missing

Article 135, VI, A, of Law No. 2026-103 (2026 Finance Act) inserts « et l'établissement public du commerce et de l'industrie de Corse » after « région » in I of article 1600 of the General Tax Code, with no end date. The version published on Légifrance for 1 January 2027 to 1 January 2028 (LEGIARTI000049534935) does not contain the insertion, while the preceding version (from 1 March 2026) and the versions for 2028 to 2030 do. The discrepancy arose in the consolidation; the text published in the Official Journal is correct.

Proposed correction

Add the insertion to the 2027 version, as in the neighbouring versions.
