ARXO-2026-0708 · Reported

Department of Commerce: 18 references to provisions removed or redesignated by 2026 rules

In the CFR chapters assigned to the Department of Commerce, 18 references in 8 sections still use an address that a 2026 final rule changed: 3 cite a section or part that a 2026 final rule removed; 15 cite a paragraph that a 2026 final rule removed or redesignated. Each rule was published in the Federal Register and in effect by October 27, 2026; the eCFR of that date keeps the old address.

Consequence

A reader who follows these references finds no text at the cited address, or text that the rule moved there from another paragraph. No effect on a decision or an amount has been established.

Proposed correction

The agency decides how to update each reference. Where the 2026 rule redesignated the paragraph, the evidence below gives the new designation.
